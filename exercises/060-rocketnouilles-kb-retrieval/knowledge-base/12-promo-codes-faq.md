# Promo codes — FAQ for participants

Happy Nouilles gives RocketNouilles users a few promo codes. Here is what participants usually ask.

## How many codes can I use on one order?

As many as you like. Every valid code you enter is applied: there is no limit and no code excludes another.

## Can I combine a percentage code with a fixed-amount code?

Yes. Percentage codes and fixed amounts all add up. For example `FIDELITE` (3 €) and `MERCI2` (2 €) together
take 5 € off, and you can add `NOUILLES5` on top when your order is at least 25 € before discounts.

## How do several percentage codes combine?

They are added together: `BIENVENUE10` and `SLURP20` give 30 % off the whole order.

## When is happy hour?

`HAPPYHOUR` gives 15 % off noodle dishes from **17:00 to 19:00** every day. It works together with every other
code, including `SLURP20`.

## Who can use ANNIV?

Anyone whose birthday is this week. Several people at the same table can use it if they all have their
birthday.

## Is RAVIOLIOFFERT really free?

Yes: one portion of Raviolis grillés is offered when you order some.

## Where do I see my discounts?

Under your cart, in the price breakdown. Rejected codes are shown with the reason.
